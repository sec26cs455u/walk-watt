⚡ AI-SMART-FOOTSTEP-ENERGY
AI Smart Footsteps to Generate Electricity | Smart FootEnergy for Smart Cities
Every step you take can light up the future. Walk Watt converts human footsteps into clean electricity using Piezoelectric Technology + AI.

🌍 Problem
India has 1.4 Billion people walking every day.
Footpaths, railway stations, schools, temples waste millions of footsteps.
Street lights use a lot of electricity from the grid.
💡 Our Solution - Smart FootEnergy
We made a smart floor tile. When you step on it, it generates electricity. AI counts steps, calculates power, and shows it live on website.

One Step = Power 1000 Steps = Charge a Mobile 1 Lakh Steps = Light a Street for 1 Night

⚙️ How It Works
Step: Person steps on the tile
Pressure: Piezoelectric sensors get pressed
Generate: Pressure -> Vibration -> Electricity
Store: Power stored in Battery / Capacitor
AI Brain: ESP32 + AI counts steps, predicts power, detects crowd
Use: Power used for LED Street Lights, Mobile Charging, Display Board
🧩 Components Used
Piezoelectric Sensors (10-20) - Generate electricity from pressure
ESP32 / Arduino Uno - Brain of project
LCD Display / OLED - Show Steps, Volts, Watts Live
Rechargeable Battery 12V - Store generated power
LED Street Light Model - Show output
Voltage Regulator, Diodes - Stabilize power
Wood / Acrylic Tile - Footstep Platform
🔋 Power Output
1 Step = 0.5 - 2 Volts
100 Steps = 5 - 10 Watts
1000 Steps = 50 - 100 Watts
1 Day in Railway Station = Enough for 10 LED Lights
🤖 AI Features
Step Counting - AI counts total footsteps
Power Prediction - Predicts how much power will generate tomorrow
Crowd Detection - Detects busy time - morning / evening
Smart Distribution - Gives more power to lights when crowd is high
Live Dashboard - Website shows live Steps, Volts, Watts
